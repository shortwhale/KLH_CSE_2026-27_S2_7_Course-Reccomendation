Specialization: Business Administration

Category:
Business

Description:
Business Administration focuses on how organizations are managed and operated. Students learn about management, marketing, finance, operations, human resources, entrepreneurship and organizational behavior. The specialization provides a broad foundation for careers in business and management.

Interests:
business, management, leadership, companies, entrepreneurship, marketing, finance, communication, decision making

Preferred School Subjects:
Commerce, Economics, Mathematics, Business Studies, Accountancy or related subjects. Students from other streams may also be eligible depending on the institution.

Skills Needed:
Leadership, Communication, Decision Making, Organization, Problem Solving, Teamwork, Analytical Thinking

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification. Specific subject requirements vary by institution.

Entrance Exams:
CUET-UG, university-specific entrance examinations and institution-specific admission processes. Requirements vary.

Duration:
Typically 3-4 years for an undergraduate degree.

Career Options:
Business Analyst, Management Trainee, Operations Executive, Marketing Executive, HR Executive, Business Development Executive, Entrepreneur

Future Scope:
Very Good. Business skills are applicable across almost every industry. Career growth can occur through specialization, professional experience, postgraduate management education and entrepreneurship.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students interested in how companies operate, leadership, communication, management, business strategy and decision making.

Specialization: Finance

Category:
Business

Description:
Finance focuses on managing money, investments, financial risk and financial resources. Students learn about accounting principles, financial markets, investment analysis, corporate finance, economics and financial planning.

Interests:
finance, money, investments, economics, stock market, banking, mathematics, analysis, business

Preferred School Subjects:
Mathematics, Economics, Accountancy, Business Studies and Commerce.

Skills Needed:
Numerical Analysis, Mathematics, Financial Analysis, Critical Thinking, Attention to Detail, Communication, Decision Making

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification. Exact requirements depend on the institution.

Entrance Exams:
CUET-UG and university-specific admission processes are common routes for relevant undergraduate programs. Requirements vary by institution.

Duration:
Typically 3-4 years for undergraduate study.

Career Options:
Financial Analyst, Investment Analyst, Banking Professional, Credit Analyst, Risk Analyst, Financial Planner, Corporate Finance Associate

Future Scope:
Excellent. Financial services are important across banking, investment management, insurance, corporate finance and government. Professional qualifications can provide additional career pathways.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students who enjoy mathematics, economics, numbers, investments, analytical thinking and understanding financial systems.

Business

economics, mathematics, statistics, business, markets, society, finance, research, data

CUET-UG and university-specific entrance examinations depending on the institution.

Duration:
Typically 3-4 years for undergraduate study.

Economic Analyst, Research Analyst, Financial Analyst, Policy Researcher, Data Analyst, Market Analyst, Economic Consultant

Very Good. Economics provides pathways into finance, consulting, policy, research, banking and analytics. Strong quantitative skills can broaden opportunities.

Recommended for students interested in mathematics, markets, finance, society, statistics, research and understanding how economic systems work.
