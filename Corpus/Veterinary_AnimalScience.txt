Specialization: Veterinary Science

Category:
Veterinary and Animal Science

Description:
Veterinary Science trains students to diagnose, treat and prevent diseases in animals, covering animal anatomy, surgery, pharmacology and public health related to livestock and pets.

Interests:
animals, medicine, biology, veterinary care, surgery, compassion

Preferred School Subjects:
Biology, Chemistry and Physics.

Skills Needed:
Animal Handling, Biology Knowledge, Compassion, Precision, Problem Solving

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification with Biology, Chemistry and Physics is required.

Entrance Exams:
NEET and institution-specific veterinary entrance examinations in India.

Duration:
Typically 5 years for a Bachelor of Veterinary Science degree.

Career Options:
Veterinarian, Animal Surgeon, Livestock Consultant, Wildlife Veterinarian

Future Scope:
Steady demand in pet care, livestock management, wildlife conservation and veterinary research.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students who love animals and want to combine that passion with a career in medicine and care.

Specialization: Animal Husbandry and Dairy Science

Category:
Veterinary and Animal Science

Description:
Animal Husbandry and Dairy Science covers the breeding, feeding, health management and productivity of livestock, with a focus on dairy and farm animal management.

Interests:
animals, farming, biology, agriculture, livestock, management

Preferred School Subjects:
Biology and Chemistry.

Skills Needed:
Animal Handling, Biology Knowledge, Farm Management, Problem Solving, Planning

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification with Biology and Chemistry is often required.

Entrance Exams:
State-level and university-specific agricultural and veterinary entrance examinations.

Duration:
Typically 4 years for a bachelor's degree in Animal Husbandry and Dairy Science.

Career Options:
Livestock Manager, Dairy Farm Manager, Animal Nutritionist, Extension Officer

Future Scope:
Consistent demand as dairy and livestock industries modernize and expand production.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students interested in farm animals, agriculture and improving livestock productivity and welfare.

Specialization: Zoology and Wildlife Studies

Category:
Veterinary and Animal Science

Description:
Zoology and Wildlife Studies focuses on the scientific study of animal behavior, physiology and ecosystems, often applied to conservation and wildlife management.

Interests:
animals, wildlife, biology, ecology, conservation, research, nature

Preferred School Subjects:
Biology and Chemistry.

Skills Needed:
Field Research, Biology Knowledge, Observation, Data Analysis, Report Writing

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification with Biology is often preferred.

Entrance Exams:
University-specific entrance examinations; some institutions admit based on merit.

Duration:
Typically 3 years for a bachelor's degree in Zoology.

Career Options:
Wildlife Biologist, Zoologist, Conservation Researcher, Wildlife Park Ranger

Future Scope:
Opportunities in conservation organizations, wildlife parks, zoos and ecological research institutions.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students fascinated by animal behavior and interested in studying and protecting wildlife.
