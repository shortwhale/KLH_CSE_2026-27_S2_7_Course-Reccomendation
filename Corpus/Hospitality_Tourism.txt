Specialization: Hotel Management

Category:
Hospitality and Tourism

Description:
Hotel Management trains students to run hotels, resorts and hospitality venues, covering front office operations, housekeeping, food and beverage service and guest relations.

Interests:
hospitality, customer service, hotels, travel, organization, management, food service

Preferred School Subjects:
Any stream; English and Business Studies where available.

Skills Needed:
Customer Service, Communication, Organization, Multitasking, Problem Solving

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream.

Entrance Exams:
NCHMCT JEE and university-specific hotel management entrance examinations in India.

Duration:
Typically 3 to 4 years for a bachelor's degree in Hotel Management.

Career Options:
Hotel Manager, Front Office Manager, Food and Beverage Manager, Guest Relations Executive

Future Scope:
Recovering and growing demand as travel and tourism expand, along with growth in luxury and boutique hospitality.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students who enjoy working with people, organizing operations and creating great guest experiences.

Specialization: Travel and Tourism Management

Category:
Hospitality and Tourism

Description:
Travel and Tourism Management covers the planning, promotion and management of travel services, tour packages and destination experiences for individuals and groups.

Interests:
travel, tourism, geography, planning, customer service, culture, communication

Preferred School Subjects:
Geography and English where available.

Skills Needed:
Planning, Communication, Cultural Awareness, Customer Service, Organization

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream.

Entrance Exams:
University-specific entrance examinations; some institutions admit based on merit.

Duration:
Typically 3 years for a bachelor's degree in Travel and Tourism Management.

Career Options:
Travel Consultant, Tour Operator, Destination Manager, Tourism Marketing Executive

Future Scope:
Growing opportunities with the expansion of domestic and international travel and experience-based tourism.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students who love exploring places, planning trips and helping others discover new destinations.
