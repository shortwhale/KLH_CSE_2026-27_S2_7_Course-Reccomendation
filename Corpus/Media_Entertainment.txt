Specialization: Film Making

Category:
Media and Entertainment

Description:
Film Making covers the art and craft of producing films, including writing, directing, cinematography, editing and sound design, for cinema, television and streaming platforms.

Interests:
film, cinema, storytelling, directing, cinematography, editing, visual storytelling

Preferred School Subjects:
English, Art and Media Studies where available.

Skills Needed:
Storytelling, Visual Composition, Editing Software, Collaboration, Creativity

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream; a portfolio or entrance test is often required.

Entrance Exams:
Institution-specific entrance examinations and portfolio reviews, such as those conducted by film institutes.

Duration:
Typically 3 years for a bachelor's degree in Film Making or a related program.

Career Options:
Film Director, Cinematographer, Film Editor, Screenwriter, Producer

Future Scope:
Growing opportunities driven by streaming platforms, regional cinema and independent film production.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students passionate about storytelling through visuals and who enjoy the technical and creative sides of filmmaking.

Specialization: Animation and VFX

Category:
Media and Entertainment

Description:
Animation and VFX focuses on creating moving images and visual effects for films, games and advertisements, combining artistic skill with technical software knowledge.

Interests:
animation, visual effects, 3d modeling, drawing, storytelling, software, design

Preferred School Subjects:
Art, Computer Science and Media Studies where available.

Skills Needed:
Drawing, 3D Software, Storyboarding, Attention to Detail, Creativity

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream; a portfolio is often required.

Entrance Exams:
Institution-specific entrance examinations and portfolio submissions.

Duration:
Typically 3 to 4 years for a bachelor's degree or diploma program.

Career Options:
Animator, VFX Artist, 3D Modeler, Motion Graphics Designer, Game Artist

Future Scope:
Strong growth in gaming, film VFX, advertising and the expanding animation content industry.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students who enjoy drawing, digital art and bringing characters and worlds to life visually.

Specialization: Journalism and Mass Communication

Category:
Media and Entertainment

Description:
Journalism and Mass Communication trains students to research, write and report news and stories across print, broadcast and digital media, along with public relations and communication strategy.

Interests:
writing, media, journalism, communication, news, reporting, interviewing

Preferred School Subjects:
English, History and Political Science where available.

Skills Needed:
Writing, Research, Interviewing, Communication, Editing

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream.

Entrance Exams:
University-specific entrance examinations; some institutions admit based on merit.

Duration:
Typically 3 years for a bachelor's degree in Journalism or Mass Communication.

Career Options:
Journalist, News Reporter, Content Writer, Editor, Public Relations Officer

Future Scope:
Evolving opportunities in digital media, podcasting, content creation and multimedia journalism.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students who enjoy writing, asking questions and telling stories that inform the public.

Specialization: Music Production

Category:
Media and Entertainment

Description:
Music Production covers composing, recording, mixing and mastering music using both traditional instruments and digital audio software, for albums, film scores and live performances.

Interests:
music, sound, composition, instruments, production, performance, audio software

Preferred School Subjects:
Music and Physics where available, for understanding sound.

Skills Needed:
Musical Ear, Audio Software, Creativity, Sound Mixing, Attention to Detail

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream; musical background or audition may be required.

Entrance Exams:
Institution-specific auditions and entrance examinations.

Duration:
Typically 3 years for a bachelor's degree or shorter for diploma programs.

Career Options:
Music Producer, Sound Engineer, Composer, Audio Editor, Live Sound Technician

Future Scope:
Expanding opportunities with streaming platforms, independent music production and film and game scoring.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students with a passion for music and sound who enjoy both the creative and technical sides of production.
