==================================================
Specialization: Fashion Design

Category:
Fashion and Textile

Description:
Fashion Design trains students to conceptualize, sketch and create clothing and accessories, covering design principles, garment construction and fashion trends.

Interests:
fashion, design, creativity, sketching, clothing, trends, sewing

Preferred School Subjects:
Art and Home Science where available.

Skills Needed:
Sketching, Creativity, Sewing Techniques, Trend Awareness, Attention to Detail

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream; a portfolio or design aptitude test is often required.

Entrance Exams:
NIFT entrance exam and institution-specific design entrance examinations in India.

Duration:
Typically 3 to 4 years for a bachelor's degree in Fashion Design.

Career Options:
Fashion Designer, Costume Designer, Stylist, Fashion Illustrator

Future Scope:
Growing opportunities in fashion houses, e-commerce brands and sustainable fashion movements.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students with a creative eye for style, clothing and self-expression through design.

Specialization: Textile Design

Category:
Fashion and Textile

Description:
Textile Design focuses on creating patterns, prints and fabric structures used in clothing and home furnishings, blending art with material science.

Interests:
textiles, design, patterns, fabric, creativity, weaving, art

Preferred School Subjects:
Art and Chemistry where available.

Skills Needed:
Pattern Design, Creativity, Material Knowledge, Attention to Detail, Software Skills

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream; a portfolio or design aptitude test is often required.

Entrance Exams:
NIFT entrance exam and institution-specific design entrance examinations in India.

Duration:
Typically 3 to 4 years for a bachelor's degree in Textile Design.

Career Options:
Textile Designer, Print Designer, Fabric Merchandiser, Surface Design Specialist

Future Scope:
Steady demand in textile manufacturing, fashion brands and interior furnishing industries.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students interested in patterns, fabric and the creative and technical sides of textile making.

Specialization: Fashion Merchandising and Retail Management

Category:
Fashion and Textile

Description:
Fashion Merchandising and Retail Management covers the business side of fashion, including buying, retail strategy, brand management and supply chain for clothing and accessories.

Interests:
fashion, business, retail, trends, marketing, planning, merchandising

Preferred School Subjects:
Any stream; Business Studies and Economics where available.

Skills Needed:
Market Analysis, Communication, Trend Forecasting, Negotiation, Planning

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream.

Entrance Exams:
NIFT entrance exam and university-specific management entrance examinations.

Duration:
Typically 3 to 4 years for a bachelor's degree in Fashion Merchandising or a related field.

Career Options:
Fashion Buyer, Retail Manager, Brand Manager, Merchandising Planner

Future Scope:
Opportunities expanding with e-commerce fashion retail and global fashion brand operations.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students who love fashion and are also interested in the business and retail strategy behind it.
