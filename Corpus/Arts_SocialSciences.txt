Specialization: Psychology

Category:
Arts and Social Sciences

Description:
Psychology is the scientific study of behavior, cognition, emotions and mental processes. Students learn about areas such as developmental psychology, social psychology, cognitive psychology, research methods and psychological assessment. Professional practice may require postgraduate education and applicable licensing or registration depending on the country and role.

Interests:
psychology, human behavior, people, emotions, mental processes, research, communication, society, biology

Preferred School Subjects:
Psychology, Biology, English, Sociology and Mathematics or Statistics where available.

Skills Needed:
Communication, Observation, Research, Critical Thinking, Empathy, Data Analysis, Listening

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification. Exact subject requirements depend on the institution.

Entrance Exams:
CUET-UG and university-specific admission processes depending on the institution.

Duration:
Typically 3-4 years for undergraduate study. Professional or specialized practice may require postgraduate education and additional training.

Career Options:
Research Assistant, HR Professional, Behavioral Researcher, Psychology Graduate roles, Mental Health Support roles where appropriately qualified. Clinical and other regulated professional roles generally require further education and applicable credentials.

Future Scope:
Very Good. Psychology knowledge is relevant to education, research, human resources, behavioral science, marketing and mental health services. Advanced professional roles generally require further qualifications.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students interested in human behavior, emotions, communication, research, observation and understanding how people think and behave.

CUET-UG and university-specific admission processes depending on the institution.

Duration:
Typically 3-4 years for undergraduate study.

Social Research Assistant, Community Program Coordinator, Policy Research Assistant, NGO Professional, Social Development Professional, Human Resources Professional

Future Scope:
