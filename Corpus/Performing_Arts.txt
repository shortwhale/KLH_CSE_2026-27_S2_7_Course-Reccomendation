CAREER RECOMMENDATION CORPUS

This corpus contains performing arts specializations for a career recommendation system.

#########################
CATEGORY: Performing Arts
#########################


==================================================
Specialization: Theatre and Drama

Category:
Performing Arts

Description:
Theatre and Drama trains students in acting, stagecraft, direction and playwriting, developing performance skills for stage, film and television.

Interests:
acting, theatre, performance, drama, storytelling, creativity

Preferred School Subjects:
English and Art where available.

Skills Needed:
Acting, Voice Projection, Memorization, Collaboration, Creativity

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream; auditions are often required.

Entrance Exams:
Institution-specific auditions and entrance examinations.

Duration:
Typically 3 years for a bachelor's degree in Theatre Arts.

Career Options:
Actor, Theatre Director, Playwright, Drama Teacher, Voice Artist

Future Scope:
Opportunities across stage theatre, television, film and the growing content and streaming industry.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students who love performing, storytelling and expressing themselves through character and voice.

Specialization: Dance

Category:
Performing Arts

Description:
Dance trains students in classical, contemporary or folk dance forms, covering technique, choreography and performance for stage and screen.

Interests:
dance, movement, performance, music, choreography, creativity

Preferred School Subjects:
Physical Education and Art where available.

Skills Needed:
Physical Fitness, Rhythm, Discipline, Creativity, Collaboration

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream; prior dance training or audition is often required.

Entrance Exams:
Institution-specific auditions and entrance examinations.

Duration:
Typically 3 years for a bachelor's degree in Dance.

Career Options:
Professional Dancer, Choreographer, Dance Instructor, Dance Therapist

Future Scope:
Opportunities in performance troupes, film and television choreography, and dance education.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students with a strong passion for movement, music and expressive physical performance.

Specialization: Fine Arts (Painting and Sculpture)

Category:
Performing Arts

Description:
Fine Arts trains students in visual art forms such as painting, sculpture and printmaking, developing technical skill and personal artistic expression.

Interests:
art, painting, sculpture, creativity, drawing, visual expression

Preferred School Subjects:
Art where available.

Skills Needed:
Drawing, Painting Techniques, Creativity, Attention to Detail, Patience

Prerequisites:
10+2 qualification in any stream; a portfolio is often required.

Entrance Exams:
Institution-specific entrance examinations and portfolio reviews.

Duration:
Typically 3 to 4 years for a Bachelor of Fine Arts program.

Career Options:
Artist, Illustrator, Art Teacher, Gallery Curator, Muralist

Future Scope:
Opportunities in galleries, public art projects, illustration and the growing digital and NFT art markets.

Why Recommended:
Recommended for students with a strong visual imagination who want to express ideas through painting, sculpture and drawing.
